PART 5 — LEARNING & PROGRESS
================================================================================

Q: How do I watch course videos?
A: After enrolling, open the course from your dashboard or courses page and
click "Start Learning." The Course Player shows the video on the left and the
lesson list on the right. Click any lesson to watch it.

Q: How is my progress tracked?
A: NeuralLearn automatically tracks:
- Which lessons you have completed
- How much time you have spent on each course
- Your overall completion percentage per course
- Quiz scores
- Your learning streak (consecutive days active)
- Total hours learned

Q: Where can I see my progress?
A: Go to Progress from the dashboard sidebar. You will see progress bars,
completion percentages, time spent, and certificates earned for every enrolled
course.

Q: What is a learning streak?
A: A streak is the number of consecutive days you have been active on the
platform. For example, a "14-day streak" means you have learned something every
day for 14 days in a row. Streaks help you build a consistent learning habit.

Q: What is the skill map?
A: The skill map is a visual breakdown of your proficiency by category based on
your course progress. For example, if you are 74% through your ML course and
89% through your React course, your skill map would show ML: 74%, Web Dev: 89%.

Q: Can I take notes while studying?
A: Yes! In the Course Player, you can write notes tied to individual lessons.
Your notes are saved and accessible whenever you revisit the lesson.

Q: What happens when I complete a course?
A: When you finish 100% of lessons in a course:
1. You are marked as "completed" in your progress
2. A certificate is issued
3. The course moves to your "Completed" section on the dashboard

Q: Does NeuralLearn have quizzes?
A: Yes. Some lessons are of type "quiz" and your quiz scores are tracked and
included in your progress analytics.

Q: What does the dashboard show?
A: Your dashboard shows:
- A personalized greeting with time of day
- 4 stat cards: Enrolled courses, Hours Learned, Completed courses, Average Progress
- Weekly activity chart (last 7 days)
- Your in-progress courses with progress bars
- Your skill map (top 5 skill categories)
- Recent community posts
- Quick action buttons to continue learning

Q: I can't log in. What should I do?
